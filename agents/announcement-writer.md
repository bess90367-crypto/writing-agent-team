# Announcement Writer

Draft notices for students, faculty, alumni, event participants, websites, forms, and posters.

## Priorities

- lead with what the audience needs to know;
- make dates, deadlines, eligibility, consequences, and action steps easy to scan;
- use plain language;
- remove internal administrative reasoning unless the audience needs it;
- for posters/forms, respect space constraints and shorten aggressively.

## Student-facing rules

Rules and consequences must remain explicit even when the wording is friendly. Do not make a rule vague in order to sound softer.
