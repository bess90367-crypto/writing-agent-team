# Good Patterns

These examples are generalized and redacted from recurring project situations. They are patterns, not templates to copy mechanically.

## A. Internal progress + necessary confirmation(s)

**Pattern**

> 老師您好：  
> 收信平安！  
>   
> 目前海報與簡報內容已完成，活動資訊也已公告。報名截止時間為［日期時間］。再請老師協助確認［所有必要的確認事項］，謝謝！

**Why it works**
- progress is stated once;
- all necessary confirmations are clear, whether one or several;
- no unnecessary history or unrequested follow-up promise.

## B. External document confirmation

**Pattern**

> 您好：  
> 收信平安！  
>   
> 我已請校內確認重新發文內容，目前草擬如下。再請協助確認院方是否還有其他需要補充或修正之處；若無問題，將依此版本辦理，謝謝！

**Why it works**
- reports what is already confirmed;
- requests one concrete check;
- closes the loop in one message.

## C. LINE scheduling

**Pattern**

> 想再跟大家確認一下，明天上午 10:00 是否方便接續討論今天尚未完成的內容？如果可以，我再依這個時間安排，謝謝～

**Why it works**
- context is one clause;
- ask appears immediately;
- tone is conversational.

## D. Vendor payment process

**Pattern**

> 收到，謝謝您提供電子發票！待收到貨後，我會依學校請款流程辦理，再由學校以電匯方式付款給貴公司。

**Why it works**
- confirms receipt;
- explains institutional sequence;
- no defensive explanation.

## E. Student rule

**Pattern**

> 請於［期限］前完成［動作］。若資料不完整，將無法保留原預約時段，需重新來信預約。

**Why it works**
- action, deadline, consequence are explicit;
- no bureaucratic filler.

## F. Ask why participation is needed

**Pattern**

> 可以，我們這邊可以協助詢問學生。想先確認一下，當天主要是有哪些內容需要學生一起了解或參與呢？我也比較好跟他們說明。

**Why it works**
- expresses willingness first;
- asks for purpose naturally;
- does not assume students must speak.
