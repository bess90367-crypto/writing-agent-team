# Recurring Work Domains

This file captures recurring communication domains so the Manager knows what information usually matters. It does not replace current-thread facts.

## Internship / hospital administration

Common tasks:
- internship applications and placement;
- contracts and amendments;
- official letters;
- insurance;
- health examination documents;
- internship fees;
- reporting/check-in requirements;
- signatures/seals and copy counts;
- confirming discrepancies between school and institution documents.

Typical information order:
1. student/unit/period if needed;
2. current document/status;
3. discrepancy or requirement;
4. action requested from the recipient;
5. deadline/next step.

High-risk details:
- exact institution name;
- internship dates;
- fee amount;
- number of contract copies;
- signature/seal requirements;
- attachment/document names.

## Course / TA / student administration

Common tasks:
- TA assignment and point allocation;
- course notices;
- attendance and assignment requirements;
- student application materials;
- borrowing/reservation rules;
- insurance reminders.

Typical information order:
1. what the notice concerns;
2. who needs to act;
3. what to submit/do;
4. deadline;
5. consequence or follow-up process.

## Faculty/internal coordination

Common tasks:
- confirm schedules;
- request names/personnel;
- report progress;
- confirm draft wording;
- ask for decisions;
- arrange meetings.

Keep background short. Internal recipients usually need the current status and the exact decision/input requested.

## Alumni / anniversary / event work

Common tasks:
- invitations;
- registration;
- volunteer coordination;
- vendor/public-relations coordination;
- event notices;
- gift/quantity/deadline messaging;
- venue and schedule updates.

Public-facing copy should be highly scannable. Internal coordination should state why a person/group needs to participate.

## Procurement / vendor / payment

Common tasks:
- quotations;
- electronic invoices;
- school reimbursement/payment flow;
- delivery confirmation;
- specification correction.

State institutional process as fact. Avoid making a vendor feel blamed for school procedure.

## Website / form / poster copy

Common tasks:
- short announcements;
- registration buttons;
- deadline labels;
- eligibility notes;
- quantity-limited notices.

Space matters. Preserve only behavior-changing facts such as dates, quantities, eligibility, reservation conditions, and links.

## Donations / fundraising / fund administration

Common tasks:
- alumni/donor fundraising notices and reports;
- donation form and supporting-document requests;
- confirming fund designation, amount, and payment details with the responsible office;
- fund-related administrative wording and coordination.

When drafting:
1. Distinguish **a document received** from **a donation, allocation, or receipt verified**.
2. If the designated fund or other transaction detail is uncertain, ask for confirmation instead of stating it as settled.
3. Mention a scan or attachment only when the user has actually provided or confirmed it.
4. Keep the question focused on the information needed for the next administrative step; do not invent receipt issuance or follow-up commitments.
